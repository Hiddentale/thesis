\begin{abstract}
    In Extremal Graph Theory, a conjecture by Babai was stated in 1978, and after some attempts disproven in 2024. This thesis gives a historical perspective of the conjecture: Which intuition gave rise to the conjecture, what progress was made over the years, and how exactly was it disproven. It intends to clarify the subject matter, giving a cohesive
    and mathematical rigorous story from start to finish.
\end{abstract}
